03Money flow

How the money moves

One launch transaction sets everything up. After that the treasury claims the rewards, funds the wallets and pays you.

OwnerYour walletSigns the launch. The only place SOL ever leaves a maker to.
launch: create, dev buy, locked split, fund ≥ 0.02 SOL
↑ payouts: your share of rewards and profit
TreasuryThe maker’s treasuryHolds capital and what it owes you. Signs its own reward claims.
fund up to the allocation, split evenly
↑ harvest profit, return unused SOL
1–10 walletsTrading walletsEach keeps its own book and lines. All listed publicly.
buy dips under their reference
↑ sell rises over their entry
pump.funThe coin’s marketBonding curve, then its PumpSwap pool after graduation.

Rewards · Every trade on a coin launched here pays creator rewards. A split locked at launch sends 80% to the treasury and 20% to Makers; nobody can change it later.

Your share · You choose how much of each claim stays in the maker and how much of each profit is reinvested. The rest is payable to you.

Losses · A loss stays with the maker’s capital. It is never taken from what you are owed, and nothing tops it up.

05Rules

The rules

Built in, not optional.

01
No wash trading
A maker’s wallets never trade against each other: none buys within a minute (or its cooldown) of another selling, and the reverse. This is enforced by the engine, not a setting.
02
Every wallet is public
The treasury and every trading wallet are listed on the maker’s page, with every trade, claim, transfer and settings change, each linked to the chain.
03
Only the owner steers
Settings, pauses and withdrawals belong to the wallet that created the maker. SOL and tokens only ever leave a maker to that wallet.
04
It can lose money
A maker buys dips that can keep dipping. Stop-loss, exposure and spending limits cap the damage; nothing removes it. Never fund one with SOL you need.

Launch with a maker, or add one to a coin you hold.

A launch takes one wallet signature. Adding a maker takes a deposit. Both can lose money.
